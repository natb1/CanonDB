# natb1/CanonDB

The principal plugin: the entry points over a hypomnemata notebook and the canon it feeds. Canon is `canon/ti-esti.md` and every answer merged since; the notebook is the `canondb` project of the one Claude Docs document `.principal/notebook.json` names. `README.md` is the projection of the two: what the plugin is for and how it is installed. Nothing of canon or of the notebook is in this file, and nothing here puts either in a turn: an entry point grants what a turn may carry, and invoking it is the grant.

- Entry points: `skills/ask`, `skills/dictate`, `skills/bootstrap`, `skills/consult` (a read-only situation read), `skills/interview` (a root question the principal answers, put by a questioner that has read nothing of the notebook), `skills/sit` (the one session that speaks with the principal; every other piece of work is a job on the board, done by a helper of a fixed kind). The canon turn is `agents/canon-answer.md`, which reads only the lineage; `hooks/canon-gate.py` refuses it every other read. Never answer a canon question outside that agent, and never work around the gate.
- The sitting's eight kinds are under `agents/`: `interviewer` (the speaking session), `coordinator` (runs the board), `clean-reader`, `grader`, `fetcher`, `recorder` (the one kind that writes the notebook), `reviewer` and `checker`; `.claude/agents/` holds byte-for-byte copies, which the gates compare a bare kind name with. Beside `hooks/canon-gate.py` are the sitting's gates, `hooks/notebook-gate.py`, `hooks/kind-bash-gate.py`, `hooks/kind-write-gate.py` and `hooks/reader-gate.py`, which share `hooks/kinds.py`. Never work around them either.
- The projections are under `tools/`: `frontier.py` (the frontier, and with `--principal` the decisions ready to be made), `again.py` (answers due again), `drift.py` (the drift count), `due.py` (what is due and why), `project.py` (the tab tree, and with `--pressure` the pressure the record derives), `residue.py` (what a line leaves open, counted; `check.py`'s `open-complete` holds each open note's parts). Each reads the document where it stands or a graph file, and stores nothing. `board.py` is the gate on the sitting's board of jobs and `views.py` lifts whole-tab reads out of a helper's transcript; `inplace.py note delete` takes a note off once what it held stands on the tab.
- Tests: `cd tools && python3 -m unittest`, before every pull request. `main` takes the user's merge: open the pull request and stop.
- A notebook write carries the rev of the read it came from; a run records itself on the notebook's `lease` tab before a section or body write; that tab's tooling pin names the plugin commit the notebook was last written with. Keep all three.
- Wording, in the notebook and here: the user, never he, his or "the human". Counts, never verdicts.
